# M2P Fintech

> M2P Fintech is Asia's largest API infrastructure company powering banking, lending, and payment solutions for fintechs, banks, NBFCs, and enterprises. Founded in 2014 and headquartered in Chennai, India, M2P operates across 30+ countries spanning Asia Pacific, MENA, NA, EU and Oceania. The platform powers 800+ fintech engagements, 200+ banks, and 300+ lenders, serving 50+ million end customers with 20+ billion transactions processed. M2P's modular, cloud-native, API-first product suite enables businesses to embed financial services — including core banking (Turing CBS), card issuance (credit, debit, prepaid, forex), UPI, cross-border payments, loan origination & management, merchant management, BNPL, microfinance, KYC (Syntizen), reconciliation (Recon360), fraud monitoring, access control, connect (orchestration platform) and neobanking — with up to 5X faster go-to-market.
